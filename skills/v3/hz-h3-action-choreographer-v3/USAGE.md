# Usage Guide: HZ MiniMax H3 Action & Combat Choreographer (V3 Production Director)

## What It Does
Includes V2's selectable choreography menus and anti-morphing beat budgets, plus **Multi-Part Fight Sequence Splitting** (>15s chained clips with frame handoffs) and an acoustic **Combat Foley Audio Dictionary**.

## Example Prompts

### 1. Multi-Part 30-Second Battle Sequence
> Use `hz-h3-action-choreographer-v3`. I want a 30-second stylized 3D fight between a knight with a greatsword and shield and an agile dual-dagger assassin. Split it into three chained 10-second clips with End-State Handoff Snapshots.

### 2. Martial Arts Exchange with Detailed Combat Foley
> Use `hz-h3-action-choreographer-v3`. Show me 4 boxing/kickboxing counter-strike options for a 12-second live-action clip, then compile the chosen option with full combat foley soundscape.
