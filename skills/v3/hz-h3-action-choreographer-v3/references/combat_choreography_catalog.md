## 1. Bladed Weapons (Katana, Longsword, Saber, Dual Daggers)

### 1A. Overhead Cleave into Cross-Guard Parry & Riposte
- **Attacker**: Steps forward with left foot, raises blade two-handed overhead, and slashes downward at a steep diagonal toward defender's left shoulder.
- **Defender**: Pivots right hip back, raises blade angled upward at 45 degrees to catch the strike near the hilt, slides the attacker's blade off to the side, and thrusts forward toward the torso.
- **Impact & Camera**: Bright metallic sparks burst at the blade junction; low-angle medium shot with a fast Push In on the blade lock, followed by `Shake Slightly` on impact.

### 1B. Low Sweep Feint into Spinning Back-Slash
- **Attacker**: Drops center of gravity into a deep lunge, sweeping the blade horizontally at knee height, then uses rotational momentum to spin 360 degrees into a high horizontal neck-level slash.
- **Defender**: Leaps cleanly over the low sweep, lands in a crouch, leans backward sharply under the spinning high slash so the blade passes inches above the chest, and kicks the attacker's planted lead knee.
- **Impact & Camera**: Dust kicks up from the floor on landing; Tracking Shot moving laterally with the spin, shifting to a tight close-up during the narrow lean-back dodge.

### 1C. Rapid Iaido Draw-Strike & Scabbard Deflection (Anime / Samurai Duel)
- **Attacker**: Holds sheathed katana at left hip in stillness, knees bent; explodes forward in a burst of speed, drawing and slashing horizontally in one continuous arc.
- **Defender**: Draws blade halfway from scabbard just in time to block the edge inches from the ribs, absorbing the force by sliding backward two meters with boots skidding across the ground.
- **Impact & Camera**: Compressed air shockwave and brief high-contrast black-and-white impact frame at contact (for Anime/Stylized 3D); Static wide shot snapping via fast Zoom In to a tight medium close-up of the locked blades.

### 1D. Dual-Dagger Close-Quarters Trap & Disarm
- **Attacker**: Lunges inside guard with a reverse-grip right-hand stab to the ribs followed immediately by a left-hand slash across the chin.
- **Defender**: Traps the stabbing wrist with left forearm, ducks under the high slash, hooks behind the attacker's elbow, and twists to force the right dagger out of the attacker's grip.
- **Impact & Camera**: Dagger clatters onto the stone floor; tight handheld-style medium shot (`Shake Slightly`) circling the combatants (`Arc Shot` with small amplitude).

## 2. Unarmed & Martial Arts (Boxing, Kickboxing, Kung Fu, Grappling)

### 2A. Jab-Cross-Hook Combo into Slip, Duck & Liver Counter
- **Attacker**: Fires a fast left jab to the head, follows with a straight right cross, and winds up a heavy left hook.
- **Defender**: Parries the jab with right palm, slips head outside the right cross, ducks cleanly beneath the whistling left hook, and drives an upward right hook directly into the attacker's exposed left ribs.
- **Impact & Camera**: Sweat droplets spray off the attacker's head on the missed hook; attacker exhales sharply and folds slightly at the waist; eye-level medium shot with dynamic whip-pan follow.

### 2B. Roundhouse Kick Catch into Sweep Takedown
- **Attacker**: Pivots on support leg and whips a high right roundhouse kick toward the defender's temple.
- **Defender**: Steps inward to jam the kick's arc, wraps left arm under the kicking leg to trap the ankle against the ribs, and sweeps the attacker's standing leg with a sharp inside low kick.
- **Impact & Camera**: Attacker's back hits the mat with a heavy thud; Pedestal Down tracking the fall from eye-level to low-angle floor level.

### 2C. Wing Chun / Kung Fu Centerline Trapping Exchange
- **Attacker**: Launches rapid straight chain punches down the centerline.
- **Defender**: Deflects each punch alternately with outward forearm parries (`Pak Sao` / `Tan Sao`), pins both of the attacker's wrists downward simultaneously, and strikes the sternum with an open palm thrust.
- **Impact & Camera**: Attacker stumbles backward three steps; crisp medium shot with fast rhythm and clean silhouette separation.

### 2D. Heavy Windup Haymaker & Shockwave Guard (Anime / Stylized 3D)
- **Attacker**: Pulls fist far back with compressed air or glowing energy swirling around the forearm, then rockets forward in a straight meteor punch.
- **Defender**: Plants both feet wide, crosses both forearms in an X-guard braced against the chest, and absorbs the direct hit.
- **Impact & Camera**: Radial dust ring blasts outward across the ground, cracking tiles beneath the defender's boots; extreme low-angle shot with `Shake Strongly` at contact followed by a slow Pull Out.

### 2E. Judo Hip Throw (O-Goshi) & Mat Slam
- **Attacker**: Charges forward attempting a wild right overhand punch.
- **Defender**: Steps inside the punch, grabs the attacker's right wrist with left hand, wraps right arm around the attacker's waist, pivots hips underneath the attacker's center of gravity, and launches the attacker overhead onto their back.
- **Impact & Camera**: Heavy body impact shakes dust off the floor; Arc Shot with medium amplitude following the rotational throw from standing to ground impact.

## 3. Firearms (Pistols, SMGs, Assault Rifles, Shotguns)

### 3A. Tactical Pistol CQB: Room Entry, Cover Dive & Return Fire
- **Attacker**: Advances with pistol in a two-handed weaver stance, firing three controlled semi-automatic shots toward the defender's corridor position.
- **Defender**: Dives sideways behind a concrete pillar as plaster chips explode from bullet impacts, leans out from the opposite side of the pillar at knee height, and fires two rapid return shots.
- **Impact & Camera**: Bright orange muzzle flashes, brass shell casings ejecting to the right, and concrete dust hanging in the air; Tracking Shot following the dive behind cover.

### 3B. Gun-Fu Disarm & Center-Axis Relock (John Wick Style)
- **Attacker**: Aims a pistol at point-blank range at the defender's chest.
- **Defender**: Slaps the pistol barrel offline to the outside with left hand while stepping inside the line of fire, grabs the slide with right hand to twist the weapon free, pivots behind the attacker's shoulder, and fires two rounds downrange at a secondary threat.
- **Impact & Camera**: Muzzle flash illuminates both faces; tight medium close-up (`Arc Shot` with small amplitude) keeping both hands and weapon crisp.

### 3C. Submachine Gun (SMG) Suppressing Burst & Sliding Counter
- **Attacker**: Sweeps a compact SMG from left to right in a sustained automatic burst, shattering glass panels and sparking off metal crates.
- **Defender**: Sprints laterally, drops into a low baseball slide across the floor beneath the bullet line, and fires an upward pistol burst from the slide toward the attacker's torso.
- **Impact & Camera**: Shower of shattered glass falling across the frame, rapid shell ejection, and sparks on metal; low-angle Truck Right matching the defender's slide speed.

### 3D. Assault Rifle Corner Pieing & Suppression Exchange
- **Attacker**: Steps around a concrete corner with an assault rifle shouldered, firing two controlled 3-round bursts down a narrow alley.
- **Defender**: Drops behind a parked car's engine block as bullets spark off the hood and shatter the windshield, pops up briefly from the trunk side, and fires three accurate return shots forcing the attacker back behind cover.
- **Impact & Camera**: Glass spiderwebs and sparks jump off metal; medium-wide over-the-shoulder shot shifting via quick Push In when return fire starts.

### 3E. Shotgun Breach & Heavy Recoil Standoff
- **Attacker**: Kicks open a wooden door, steps through, and fires a pump-action shotgun at a heavy barricade, racking the pump immediately afterward.
- **Defender**: Drops prone behind an overturned steel table as buckshot dents the metal, rolls to the flank, and fires a controlled rifle burst across the doorway.
- **Impact & Camera**: Heavy recoil kicks the shotgun barrel upward, spent red shell ejects cleanly; medium-wide shot with `Shake Slightly` on each blast.

## 4. Polearms, Heavy Melee & Battlefield Clashes

### 4A. Spear Thrust Deflection into Staff Sweep
- **Attacker**: Drives a long spear forward in a two-handed straight thrust toward the defender's chest.
- **Defender**: Slaps the spear shaft downward with the butt of a wooden/iron staff, steps forward along the shaft to close distance, and swings the opposite end of the staff horizontally into the attacker's shoulder.
- **Impact & Camera**: Sharp wood-on-metal clack and dust burst; medium-wide Tracking Shot emphasizing weapon length and spatial distance.

### 4B. Greatsword / Battleaxe Heavy Swing vs. Shield Bash
- **Attacker**: Swings a heavy two-handed greatsword in a wide horizontal arc with full body rotation.
- **Defender**: Steps forward into the swing with a round metal shield raised, absorbs the heavy blow on the shield face, and immediately lunges forward with a shield bash into the attacker's chest.
- **Impact & Camera**: Deep metallic boom, sparks showering downward, attacker knocked off balance; low-angle hero shot with fast Push In.

### 4C. War Hammer Overhead Smash & Roll Counter
- **Attacker**: Leaps forward and brings a massive two-handed war hammer crashing downward onto the ground.
- **Defender**: Executes a low forward shoulder roll to the left just before impact, comes up in a kneeling stance beside the attacker's flank, and drives a short-blade thrust toward the unguarded side.
- **Impact & Camera**: Stone slabs shatter and dust erupts upward from the hammer strike; low-angle wide shot with `Shake Strongly` on ground impact.

## 5. Magic, Supernatural & Energy Clashes (Anime / Fantasy / Sci-Fi)

### 5A. Energy Beam / Fireball Deflection with Bladed Arc
- **Attacker**: Thrusts both palms forward, launching a concentrated blazing energy sphere across the arena.
- **Defender**: Plants feet wide, swings an energy-infused blade upward in a crescent slash, slicing the projectile cleanly in half so both halves detonate harmlessly against the side walls.
- **Impact & Camera**: Bright volumetric glow illuminates both fighters, scorch marks and smoke billow on the side walls; dynamic Arc Shot rotating 90 degrees around the defender.

### 5B. Blink-Step Teleport Ambush & Barrier Counter
- **Attacker**: Vanishes in a brief motion-blur dash from 10 meters away and reappears directly behind the defender with a downward strike.
- **Defender**: Turns head slightly and raises one hand backward, summoning a translucent hexagonal energy shield that catches the strike inches from the spine, then blasts a shockwave pulse outward.
- **Impact & Camera**: Rippling energy fractures across the shield, attacker launched backward skidding across the ground; fast Whip Pan / Zoom Out on impact.

## 6. Cartoon & Slapstick Combat (2D Cartoon / Stylized Comedy)

### 6A. Exaggerated Windup Haymaker & Accordion Dodge
- **Attacker**: Winds right arm backward like a spinning propeller before launching a comically oversized punch.
- **Defender**: Squashes down vertically like an accordion so the punch whistles right over their head, then springs upward with a comically giant wooden mallet bonk on the attacker's helmet.
- **Impact & Camera**: Classic stars/rings spin briefly above the attacker's head as they wobble on rubbery knees; Static medium shot with snappy timing and clear silhouette poses.

## 7. Medium-Specific Styling Modifiers

Append the matching visual modifier to `integrated_multimodal_description` or `detailed_description`:

- **Live-Action Photorealistic**: `Photorealistic 35mm cinema look, natural motion blur, realistic skin and fabric physics, grounded center of gravity, authentic weapon weight and recoil.`
- **2D Anime / Sakuga**: `High-budget 2D anime action animation, crisp cel-shaded linework, dynamic foreshortening, brief high-contrast impact frames at the exact moment of strike, dramatic wind trails and debris.`
- **Cartoon / Western 2D**: `Expressive 2D hand-drawn cartoon animation, bold silhouettes, clear anticipation windups, snappy smear frames during fast strikes, exaggerated comic recoil.`
- **Stylized 3D**: `High-end stylized 3D CG animation, PBR materials, sharp rim lighting separating combatants from the background, fluid pose-to-pose action arcs, kinetic camera tracking.`

## 8. Combat Foley Audio Dictionary (For `overall_soundscape`)

Use concrete acoustic descriptors rather than vague labels:

- **Blades**: `Sharp steel-on-steel ring, metallic scrape along the blade edge, rapid air whoosh on swings, leather scabbard click, boots skidding across gravel.`
- **Martial Arts**: `Crisp cloth snap on fast punches, dull heavy thud of body impacts, sharp exhalations, sneakers squeaking on the mat, heavy breathing between exchanges.`
- **Firearms**: `Dry, echoing gunshots, metallic slide reciprocation, tinkling brass casings hitting concrete, splintering wood and shattering glass, heavy tactical footsteps.`
- **Heavy Melee & Energy**: `Deep resonant iron clang, shattering stone debris, low sub-bass air compression rumble, crackling electrical discharge.`
