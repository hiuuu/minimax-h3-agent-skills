# Usage Guide: HZ MiniMax H3 Action & Combat Choreographer (V2)

## What It Does
Presents a structured menu of **3 to 5 cinematic Attack -> Defense -> Counter -> Camera exchanges** tailored to your weapon and visual medium (Live-Action, 2D Anime, Cartoon, Stylized 3D), then compiles your choice into an official MiniMax H3 prompt.

## Example Prompts

### 1. Browse Sword Duel Choreography (Anime Style)
> Use `hz-h3-action-choreographer-v2`. I want a 10-second 2D anime katana duel between two samurai in a bamboo forest. Show me choreography options first so I can choose.

### 2. Tactical Gun-Fu Shootout (Live-Action)
> Use `hz-h3-action-choreographer-v2`. Design a close-quarters pistol disarm and cover shootout in photorealistic live-action style for an 8-second I2VA clip starting from `<Picture 1>`.
